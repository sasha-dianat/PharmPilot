# Known Gaps and Risks

Severity reflects potential impact if the current snapshot were exposed to real users or data. Confidence refers to the static evidence, not exploitability in an unknown deployment.

| Severity | Confidence | Gap or contradiction | Evidence | Impact | Recommended disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | High | Identity routes trust caller-supplied pharmacy IDs and unscoped patient/link IDs | `services/platform/routers/identity.py` | Authenticated staff can operate on another pharmacy’s identity graph or candidates | Remove tenant input, derive scope from staff, add pharmacy-bound loaders and negative tests |
| Critical | High | Insurer sandbox fallback synthesizes plausible demographics and family members; the orchestrator may persist them | `services/integrations/iranian_insurance/adapters.py:_SandboxFallbackAdapter`; `identity_orchestrator.py:_upsert_patient` | Fictitious patients, insurance rows, and family links can be created when credentials are absent or a live service fails | Never fallback a live failure to fake data; label provenance; prohibit sandbox persistence outside explicit demo fixtures |
| Critical | High | Unauthenticated clinical review accepts arbitrary Rx, patient, and pharmacy IDs | `services/platform/routers/clinical_brain.py:review_prescription` | Cross-tenant PHI access and possible external AI disclosure | Disable until authenticated and tenant-bound; preferably consolidate with the governed clinical command path |
| Critical | High | Audio, biometric, and security edge ingest plus Rx/biometric/security WebSockets are unauthenticated or unbound | `audio.py:transcribe_recording`; `biometric.py`; `security_events.py:ingest_behavior_detection`; `prescriptions.py:rx_queue_websocket` | PHI disclosure, forged events, biometric enrollment, tenant surveillance, and resource exhaustion | Add service/staff authentication, pharmacy binding, replay protection, rate limits, and payload limits |
| Critical | High | Tenant checks are missing across patient and Rx child routes | `patients.py` allergy/insurance/lab/note routes; `prescriptions.py` fills, transitions, history, claim/release paths | Cross-pharmacy read and mutation of PHI and workflow state | Centralize parent loaders and add two-pharmacy HTTP tests |
| Critical | High | Rx intake can link a Pharmacy A prescription to a Pharmacy B patient | `services/platform/routers/prescriptions.py:intake_prescription` | Cross-tenant clinical association and downstream PHI leakage | Validate patient ownership before creating the Rx |
| Critical | High | Claims load fills, Rx, patients, and insurance by ID only; history accepts arbitrary pharmacy | `services/platform/routers/adjudication.py` | Cross-tenant claim submission, reversal, and financial/PHI access | Bind the complete aggregate to `staff.pharmacy_id` and verify insurance belongs to the same patient |
| Critical | High | Clinical council, SSE council, and knowledge query patient context are unscoped | `services/platform/routers/phase32.py`; `knowledge.py:query_knowledge_base` | Cross-tenant allergies, labs, diagnoses, medications, and AI egress | Use tenant-bound clinical context loaders and authorize Rx-patient pairing |
| Critical | High | Prescription documents have no pharmacy column or Rx-ownership check | `services/platform/routers/rx_documents.py` | Any authenticated staff can list/download/delete another tenant’s scans | Migrate to tenant-aware metadata and storage; authorize every operation through the owning Rx |
| High | High | Document `rx_id` is an unrestricted string used in a filesystem path | `rx_documents.py:_doc_dir`, `_save_file` | Authenticated path traversal and writes outside the intended document root | Require UUIDs, resolve and verify containment, use managed object storage |
| Critical | High | Transcription accepts arbitrary document/patient/Rx IDs and an optional pharmacy override | `services/platform/routers/rx_transcription.py` | Cross-tenant OCR, person linking, spoofed audit actors, and unscoped event enumeration | Bind documents and all patient/Rx IDs to staff pharmacy; derive actor from auth; migrate schema |
| High | High | DUR override records are global, caller-supplied, and disconnected from server findings | `services/platform/routers/dur_overrides.py` | Fabricated or cross-tenant legal/clinical audit evidence | Store foreign keys, pharmacy and staff identity; validate alert and Rx; make records immutable |
| Critical | High | Interaction acknowledgement accepts an arbitrary hash and an empty acknowledgement list; state transitions ignore it | `services/platform/routers/cds.py:acknowledge_interactions`; workstation `VerificationCenter.tsx` | Serious interaction gates can be bypassed | Validate current report server-side and make exact acknowledgement a transition prerequisite |
| Critical | High | POS directly sets prescriptions to dispensed and does not tenant-scope Rx or payment records | `services/platform/routers/pos.py` | Audit-chain bypass, cross-tenant dispense, false payment history | Replace with a tenant-bound atomic finalization command through `RxStateMachine` |
| High | High | POS queries a table not found in models or migrations and uses US/float money semantics | `pos.py:_get_rx_row`; no `adjudication_results` definition found | Runtime failure or zero patient-pay; incorrect Iranian receipts | Model the authoritative payment/claim source and use integer/Decimal Rial |
